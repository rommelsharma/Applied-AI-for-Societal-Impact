Core Learnings
• Two judges can evaluate the same case differently for reasons unrelated to the merits of the case, and
that variability is itself a serious error source.
• Judgment quality problems are not exhausted by bias; unwanted inconsistency can create unfairness,
financial loss, and governance failure.
• Noise often remains invisible until organizations deliberately measure it.
• Decision hygiene reduces error by improving process structure rather than by trying to fix every bias
directly in the moment.
• Independent judgments, structured criteria, aggregation, and the outside view are recurring methods for
improving reliability.
Directions, Observations, And Implications
