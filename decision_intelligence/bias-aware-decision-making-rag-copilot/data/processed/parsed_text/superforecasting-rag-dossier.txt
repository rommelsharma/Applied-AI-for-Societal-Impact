Core Learnings
• Most experts are poor forecasters, but some individuals consistently outperform by using disciplined
habits rather than special intuition.
• Good forecasting is iterative: break problems down, estimate in probabilities, update often, and revise
when new evidence appears.
• Strong forecasters combine open-mindedness with skepticism, humility, and comfort with uncertainty.
• Forecast accuracy improves when judgments are calibrated and numerically explicit rather than vague
and categorical.
• The book helps shift decisions from confident storytelling toward evidence-weighted probabilistic
reasoning.
