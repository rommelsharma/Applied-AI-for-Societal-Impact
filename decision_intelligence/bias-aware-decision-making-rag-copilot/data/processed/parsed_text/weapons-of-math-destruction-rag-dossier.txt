Core Learnings
• Scalable models can amplify bias when they are opaque, unregulated, and hard for affected people to
contest.
• Algorithmic systems are often presented as objective while embedding choices about data, proxies,
optimization, and power.
• High-stakes systems in lending, hiring, policing, education, and insurance can reinforce inequality
instead of reducing it.
• The combination of scale, opacity, and weak accountability is especially dangerous in public or
quasi-public decision systems.
• The book is valuable because it makes systemic harm concrete and institutionally legible.
